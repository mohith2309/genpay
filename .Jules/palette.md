## 2026-07-02 - Add tooltip to SMS Review IconButton
**Learning:** IconButtons used for reviewing or processing background events (like SMS parsing) need clear tooltips describing the action ('Review pending SMS transactions'), as the icon alone (like an SMS bubble) may simply imply 'Messages' rather than an actionable review queue.
**Action:** Always add descriptive tooltips to IconButtons, especially those triggering secondary workflows or review screens.
