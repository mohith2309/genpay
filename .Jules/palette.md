## 2024-06-28 - Swipe Actions Accessibility & Haptics in TransactionCard
**Learning:** In custom swipe-to-reveal components like `TransactionCard`, using a raw `GestureDetector` instead of `Dismissible` loses native accessibility and haptic feedback. Wrapping the widget in a `Semantics` widget with `customSemanticsActions` provides screen reader users with direct access to the swipe menu actions (Silent, Delete, Repeat) without needing to perform the swipe gesture. Adding haptic feedback makes the interaction more tactile and satisfying.
**Action:** When implementing custom swipe menus, always add `customSemanticsActions` and haptics. Also ensure action buttons explicitly have `HitTestBehavior.opaque` to receive taps reliably and reset interaction flags (like `_isRevealing`) inside the callbacks.
